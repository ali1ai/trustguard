# TrustGuard Demo Community Content Policy

**Version:** 2026-10-25-demo-v2  
**Status:** Illustrative policy for the portfolio project, not legal advice or a production policy.

## Spam and scams
Repetitive, deceptive, unsolicited promotional content, suspicious bulk solicitation, impersonation,
phishing attempts, and scam-like promises may violate policy. A URL by itself is not sufficient evidence.

## Harassment
Targeted insults, degrading attacks, or repeated unwanted abusive contact may violate policy. Criticism,
content moderation appeals, and disagreement are not automatically harassment.

## Hate
Attacks or dehumanization aimed at a protected group may violate policy. Identity words or neutral discussion
of a protected group are not, by themselves, violations.

## Threats and violence
Credible statements expressing intent to physically harm a person or group require urgent review. Quoting,
condemning, reporting, or discussing a threat for educational or journalistic purposes is context-dependent.

## Sexual and self-harm-related content
Apply the host platform's relevant policy and escalation process. The demo does not implement specialized
child-safety detection or emergency response and must not be used as a substitute for those systems.

## General toxicity signal
A broad toxicity classifier may flag uncivil or insulting language, but a dataset-level toxicity label is not interchangeable with harassment, hate, threats, or a confirmed policy violation. Use it as a triage signal, consider context, and send ambiguous/high-impact cases to review.

## Context, uncertainty, and enforcement
When the system cannot distinguish violation from quotation, counterspeech, education, support, satire, or
reclaimed language, route the case to a human reviewer. The system may recommend allow, label, reversible
soft-hold, or review. It cannot ban accounts or permanently delete content. Review decisions must be logged.
